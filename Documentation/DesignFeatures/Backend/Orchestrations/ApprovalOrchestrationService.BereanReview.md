# Approval orchestration service — Berean's review
Parent: [BereanReview.md](../../BereanReview.md)
Level: orchestration — `IApprovalOrchestrationService` (`Glory2Him.Core/Services/Orchestrations/Approvals/`). The Likes feature's work on the same service is `Backend/Orchestrations/ApprovalOrchestrationService.md`, and the amender record's `ApprovalOrchestrationService.Amenders.md`.
Inherits: §APR8.6 HR-4, §APR8.8, §APR9.7.3, §APR9.7.4, §APR9.7.7, §ARC12.5.3, §ARC16.7.1, §ARC16.7.5, §EVN18(a), (d) and (h), §EVN20 rule 4, §SEC14.6.1, BereanReview.md rules 4, 9–11, 13, 14, 19, 20, 22 and 23

The round's rules for Berean, and the approval process's step 4. The service adds no dependency: it clears an item's score through the item's `<Entity>-SettingConfidence` request door, as it decides an item through `<Entity>-Approving`, and it publishes the review event, both with the `IEventBroker` it holds. It dismisses, clears, assigns and removes Berean through the `IAIReviewerAssignmentWorkflowService` seam it holds, and it reads through the `IAccessBroker` it holds. It keeps its three entity services.

**Order in the Modified flow.** Every dismissal the flow makes comes before every write it makes that the round re-tests, so that no re-test inside the flow counts a review the flow is about to dismiss. The re-tests are not suppressed, because §EVN18(d)'s window is the review dismissal's loop alone; the order is what makes them safe.

1. **The amender's dismissal comes first**, once the round is resolved and before the round follows the entity (`ApprovalOrchestrationService.Amenders.md §1` rule 2).
2. **The dismissal of the round's active reviews under `RequireReapprovalOnChange = true`** (§APR8.8 rule 1), **and of Berean's vote alone under `false`** (§2 rule 4), come before §2 rule 3's two writes and §8's assignment or removal. The assignment's writes each publish a fact the round re-tests — the dismissal's `AIReviewerAssignment-Removed`, the return's `-Modified`, §8's `-Added`, and the `-Removed` of §8's removal (§4–§6). The score's clear publishes `<Entity>-ConfidenceSet`, which nothing hears (BereanReview.md, *Events*), so no re-test runs inside it; it comes after the dismissals all the same, because the evaluation reads the score it clears (rule 4).
3. **The round's move between `Draft` and `Submitted` publishes `Approval-Modified`**, which the automatic assignment hears, and assigns Berean inside, until its subscription is retired (`Backend/Orchestrations/AIReviewerOrchestrationService.BereanReview.md §6`). The three ears (§4–§6) are wired only once both of its subscriptions are retired, so that no re-test runs inside the move — which comes before the review dismissal under `true` — nor inside the reset's write of the round's status, which comes before the reset's dismissal (§ARC16.7.5).
4. **The flow's own evaluation reads the round's conditions after its last write**, under either setting, so that it decides on what its writes left: the reviews dismissed, the score cleared, Berean returned to pending, dismissed, assigned or removed, and the blocks each raises or lifts. Under `true` the flow already reads them again after the review dismissal, before it evaluates (`ApprovalOrchestrationService.Flows.cs`, *RE-READ*), and §2 rule 3's writes and §8's step land before that read. Under `false` the flow today evaluates on the conditions it read to learn the setting, a read that comes before §2 rule 3's writes and §8's step; so it reads them again after those writes, and evaluates on that read. **§8 adds the second read** (#1225), because it is the first of the two to write on that path after the first read, and §2 keeps it (#1167). The single read's pin, `ShouldEvaluateOnTheConditionsAlreadyReadWhenTheStaleReviewResetIsOffAsync` (`ApprovalOrchestrationServiceTests.Flows.cs`), goes with it.

So by the time any re-test runs inside the flow, every review the flow dismisses is dismissed, and what it counts is what the flow's own evaluation then counts, since that evaluation reads the round after the flow's writes (rule 4). Under `false` that includes the people's standing reviews, which the flow's own evaluation counts too. Without rule 4, once the gathers for Berean's blocks are built (`Backend/Brokers/AccessBroker.BereanReview.md §4`), the evaluation under `false` would run on conditions read before Berean was assigned or returned to pending, and could approve a round whose people's standing reviews meet the count while the re-tests inside the flow held it for Berean (BereanReview.md rules 9 step 4.4 and 11). Without the order, an amender's approval meeting a count of one under `false` would be counted by a re-test inside the move, before the amender's dismissal ran, and could approve the round on the amender's own vouch (§APR8.6 HR-5).

## 1. ResetApprovalAsync — Berean's review left as it is (#1166)

```csharp
ValueTask<ApprovalOutcome> ResetApprovalAsync(
    EntityType entityType,
    Guid entityId,
    CancellationToken cancellationToken = default);
```

1. **The reset no longer returns Berean's assignment to pending** (`ApprovalOrchestrationService.Resets.cs`, the call to `ResetStaleAIReviewerAssignmentAsync`). It re-opens the same content, which is no data change, so Berean's assignment, its tracking fields and the item's score stand on the re-opened round (BereanReview.md rules 7, 9 and 23; I1.4, I2.D, R5). Everything else the reset does is unchanged.
2. **The reset fires nothing of Berean's** (BereanReview.md rule 23; R4): it assigns no Berean and publishes no review event. Its command's `-Submitted`, under the system identity, never reaches step 4, because every ear of the Modified flow drops a fact carrying that identity (§ARC16.7.5), and that guard stays (§8 rule 2). *(This rule used to have the re-opening reach step 4, which published the review event so that Berean voted again on its standing score.)*
3. **The reset's dismissal spares Berean's vote** (BereanReview.md rule 23; R5: *"Every human reviewer is dismissed. Only Berean remains unchanged."*), which narrows §APR8.8 regardless-rule 1 for Berean alone. No round holds a vote of Berean's until the review process casts one, so the exemption is built with the vote, under #1216, as §2 rule 4's dismissal of Berean's vote alone is; this section builds none of it, and the reset's dismissal is unchanged until then. **The exemption makes untrue every word that says the reset dismisses every review**, and the task #1216 carves for it rewrites them, so that no administrator is told every recorded review is gone while Berean's vote stands and can approve the round again (BereanReview.md, *Risks*): the review panel's reset confirmation, which an administrator reads before resetting, *"This item will go back to Submitted for review and every recorded review will be dismissed. It will be removed from the public site until it is approved again."* (`reviewPanel.tsx`, `resetConfirmMessageText`); the reset's success toast on the post page, *"The approval has been reset. The post is back with the reviewers, and its recorded reviews have been dismissed."* (`contentItemModerationDetailPage.tsx`), and the association page's, designed in its words (`UI/Pages/AssociationModerationDetailPage.md` rule 2.19); the comment over the vote's refusal on the post page, *"every active review is dismissed"* (`contentItemModerationDetailPage.tsx`); `approvalService.ts`'s *"its reviews are dismissed"*; the comment over the reset's dismissal, *"An override ALWAYS dismisses the active reviews"* (`ApprovalOrchestrationService.Resets.cs`); `IApprovalOrchestrationService.ResetApprovalAsync`'s *"Every active review is dismissed"*; and the reset route's summary, *"dismisses every active review"* (`ApprovalsController.cs`). Until then each stays true.

## 2. ProcessEntityModifiedAsync — the data change (#1167)

```csharp
ValueTask<ApprovalOutcome> ProcessEntityModifiedAsync(
    EntityType entityType,
    Guid entityId,
    CancellationToken cancellationToken = default);
```

1. **Steps 1–3 run only on a data change** (BereanReview.md rule 9). A data change here is a `-Modified` of `ContentItem`, `Link`, `Tag`, `Reaction`, `Comment` or `BibleReference` whose write changed a content field, by the test §APR8.6.3 rule 3 applies to the amender record. An echo of the stored row is none, and neither is a status move between `Draft` and `Submitted` alone (BereanReview.md R1). The `-Submitted` ears, `Association-Modified` and `Association-Repointed` carry none. A redelivered `-Modified` runs them again, as it runs the review dismissal again (§APR9.7.4, *One residual*). **How each `-Modified` ear learns whether its fact's write changed a field, and hands that to the flow, is designed under #1215**, because the fact carries the row after the write and never the row before it. This section's task (#1167) is reworked and carried under #1215, and waits for that design on `main` (BereanReview.md, *What ships together* step 6).
2. **Before the round follows the entity, the live assignment is read.** The flow reads the round's live Berean assignment, if one stands, through `IAccessBroker.RetrieveLiveAIReviewerAssignmentAsync` (`Backend/Brokers/AccessBroker.BereanReview.md §1`) — never a caller-facing read, because the flow runs as the editor — before it moves a `Draft` round to `Submitted` with the entity (§APR9.2 rule 3). That is the assignment rule 3 acts on. One the round gains during the flow is the new round's and stands: until the automatic assignment's subscription on `Approval-Modified` is retired (`Backend/Orchestrations/AIReviewerOrchestrationService.BereanReview.md §6`), it hears the move's `Approval-Modified` inside the move's publish, because delivery is synchronous (§EVN11), so on a first submission through the general modify it has already assigned Berean by the time the flow reaches rule 3 (BereanReview.md rule 9).
3. **Before the round is evaluated, and after the flow's dismissal of the round's active reviews under `RequireReapprovalOnChange = true`** (§APR8.8 rule 1), which dismisses Berean's vote with them where it cast one, **two writes, in this order**, each under `CreateSystemAsync` (BereanReview.md rule 19):
   1. **the score, under either setting.** Where the item holds a score — read through `IAccessBroker.RetrieveApprovalSubjectConfidenceAsync` (`Backend/Brokers/AccessBroker.BereanReview.md §3`) — its four `IConfidence` fields are cleared, by publishing `<Entity>-SettingConfidence` with all four null and awaiting the reply (rule 9, step 2). The address is each item's door (`Backend/Foundations/<Entity>Service.BereanReview.md §4`). An item with no score is sent nothing;
   2. **the assignment rule 2 read, if one stood and is still live.** Under `true` it is dismissed through `IAIReviewerAssignmentWorkflowService.DismissStaleAIReviewerAssignmentAsync`, which clears its five tracking fields in the same write (`Backend/Foundations/AIReviewerAssignmentService.md §2`; rule 9, steps 1 and 3). Under `false` it is returned to pending through `IAIReviewerAssignmentWorkflowService.ReturnStaleAIReviewerAssignmentToPendingAsync`, which clears its five tracking fields and leaves it assigned (`Backend/Foundations/AIReviewerAssignmentService.md §5`; rule 9, step 3).

   **The clear comes first so that no review can skip over a stale score.** The review process skips the scoring where a score stands (BereanReview.md rule 6); with the assignment cleared and the old score still standing, a fresh review would record nothing over the changed content. A failure of either write faults the flow, as a failure of the review dismissal before them does, so the evaluation never runs over a score or an assignment that describes content that has gone, and step 4 (§8) does not run. Nothing in this host delivers the fact again (§EVN23 rule 6):
   - **a clear that fails** leaves the old score and Berean's assignment as they were, over content that has changed. Under `true` the people's reviews are already dismissed, so the count holds the round as it does today. Under `false` the people's reviews stand, and the old score keeps holding the round where it is low and Berean is assigned (rule 10) and blocks nothing where it is not. The next data change clears it;
   - **an assignment write that fails after the clear** leaves Berean's assignment as it was over an item with no score. A completed review is then held by the low-score block where it is on, since a null score reads as `0.00` (rule 10), and a review not yet completed by the pending or in-flight block (rule 11). The next data change clears it.

   **The dismissal's `AIReviewerAssignment-Removed`, and the return's `-Modified`, are re-tested inside the flow, and the order is what makes that safe** (*Order in the Modified flow*). The round's ears on those addresses (§5, §6) run inside the write's publish, and §EVN18(d)'s suppression does not cover them, because its window is the review dismissal's loop alone. By the time either runs, every review the flow dismisses is dismissed — the amender's, the round's under `true`, and Berean's vote under `false` — and the score is cleared, so the re-test counts only what the flow's own evaluation then counts. *(This paragraph used to say that the re-tests were harmless because the reviews were dismissed, which held under `true` alone.)*
4. **Under `RequireReapprovalOnChange = false`, Berean's vote alone is dismissed** where it cast one (BereanReview.md rule 9, step 1; I5.2), before rule 3's two writes and §8's assignment (*Order in the Modified flow*). That dismissal is built with the vote, under #1216, which designs how the flow finds Berean's vote; this section builds none of it.
5. **The return to pending's other path goes** from this flow: the call under `RequireReapprovalOnChange = true` on every dismissing `-Modified` (`ApprovalOrchestrationService.Flows.cs`, its AI half), which rule 3 replaces. The helper `ResetStaleAIReviewerAssignmentAsync` goes once §1 has removed its other call, and its gather with it (`Backend/Brokers/AccessBroker.BereanReview.md §2`); so does `ApprovalOrchestrationService.AIReviewer.cs`, whose only member it is, with its class summary. Every comment that names the helper as a precedent or a sibling — the contained delivery reports of the seven foundations' transitions and of `ApprovalOrchestrationService.Decisions.cs`, and the tests that set a posture beside it — is rewritten without it. The comment over the flow's AI half is rewritten to BereanReview.md rule 9.
6. **Step 4 then runs as §8 has it**: it finds the assignment rule 3 dismissed gone, and assigns Berean afresh where the policy asks for it automatically, or the assignment rule 3 returned to pending standing, and publishes the event for it, or removes it where the round no longer offers Berean (§8 rule 1; R9). Rule 3's two writes come before step 4's read of the live assignment, so this section orders them before §8's step, which lands first.
7. **The evaluation reads the conditions after rule 3's writes**, under either setting (*Order in the Modified flow* rule 4). Under `false` that is §8's second read, which this section keeps and lands its writes before; under `true` it is the read after the review dismissal.

## 7. ProcessEntityAddedAsync — step 4: Berean assigned and its review started (#1224)

```csharp
ValueTask<ApprovalOutcome> ProcessEntityAddedAsync(
    EntityType entityType,
    Guid entityId,
    CancellationToken cancellationToken = default);
```

1. **Where the round is created at `Submitted`, before it is evaluated**, the flow resolves Berean's policy for it through `IAccessBroker.ResolveAIReviewerPolicyByIdAsync` (BereanReview.md rule 9, step 4). A round created at `Draft` ends the flow as it does today (§APR9.7.3 rule 1), and this section runs nothing on it.
2. **Where `IsAutomaticallyRequested` is `true` and no live assignment stands**, read through `IAccessBroker.RetrieveLiveAIReviewerAssignmentAsync`, Berean is assigned through `IAIReviewerAssignmentWorkflowService.AddAutomaticAIReviewerAssignmentAsync` (step 4.2; I4.2). `IsAutomaticallyRequested` carries the offer and a round that requires review (`Backend/Clients/AccessClient.BereanReview.md §3`), so a round that requires no review is assigned nothing, and is approved by the evaluation that follows (rule 22). Where a live assignment already stands, nothing is added: until the subscription on `Approval-Added` is retired (`Backend/Orchestrations/AIReviewerOrchestrationService.BereanReview.md §5`), it has assigned Berean inside the round's own publish (§EVN11).
3. **Where a live assignment then stands**, read again once rule 2 has run, and the verdict rule 1 resolved has `IsOffered` `true`, `AIReviewerOrchestration-Reviewing` is published once, carrying it, under `CreateSystemAsync` (step 4.3; BereanReview.md rule 4; I5.1, I6, I7). **Where one stands and `IsOffered` is `false`** — the round no longer offers Berean (`Backend/Clients/AccessClient.BereanReview.md §3`) — nothing is published, and the assignment is removed through `IAIReviewerAssignmentWorkflowService.RemoveUnofferedAIReviewerAssignmentAsync` (`Backend/Foundations/AIReviewerAssignmentService.md §7`), which mints the system identity itself (BereanReview.md rule 9, step 4.3; R9: *"Don't fire, and remove Berean"*). Rule 2 never assigns Berean there, since `IsAutomaticallyRequested` carries the offer, so the assignment removed is one that stood before the flow: on this flow, one a person's request made before a redelivered `-Added` (rule 6), while the round still offered Berean. Where none stands, nothing is published (I6) and nothing is removed. The address is minted by `Backend/Orchestrations/AIReviewerOrchestrationService.BereanReview.md §2`.
4. **The round is then evaluated as today** (§APR9.7.3 rule 3, §APR9.7.7), and only a round no condition blocks is approved (step 4.4). The flow's evaluation already reads the round's conditions when it runs (`EvaluateResolvedApprovalAsync`), so it reads them after rules 2 and 3, and sees the assignment rule 2 made, or the removal rule 3 made.
5. **A failure of the assignment faults the flow before the evaluation**, so the round stays `Submitted` and unevaluated: the policy requires Berean (I2.B), and a round evaluated without it on such a tier is one Berean was meant to hold. *(The automatic assignment this replaces logged its failure and let the round proceed, §APR8.6.2.1, Failure posture, because a missed assignment then cost one click.)* Nothing else holds such a round, as the owner ruled (BereanReview.md R7), and BereanReview.md, *Risks*, records it. **A collision is not a failure.** Where the add is refused because a live assignment was written in the same moment — a person's request, or a second delivery's flow — `UX_AIReviewerAssignments_ApprovalId` refuses the loser, and the seam surfaces it as an `AIReviewerAssignmentDependencyValidationException` (`IAIReviewerAssignmentWorkflowService.cs`, the automatic add's summary). Where the add answers that exception and rule 3's read then finds a live assignment, Berean stands assigned, so the flow goes on: it publishes the event for that assignment and evaluates the round, as §APR8.6.2.1's *two deliveries at once* contained the same race (§ARC12.5.4 business rule 4; #479). Where the read finds none, the exception faults the flow as any other failure of the add does. **A failure of the publish is logged and the flow goes on**, as §2 rule 4 of the AI reviewer story has it: Berean is assigned, its review has not started, and the pending block holds the round (BereanReview.md rule 11) until a person asks for Berean again, which publishes it (I7). **A failure of the removal faults the flow before the evaluation**, as a failure of the assignment does, and nothing is published: Berean stays assigned, and the round stays `Submitted` and unevaluated, held by Berean's blocks once their gathers ship, until a person's next update runs step 4 again.
6. **It records no `ProcessedEvent`**, as the flow does not (§EVN19 rules 1 and 4). A redelivered `-Added` finds the live assignment rule 2 made, adds nothing, and publishes the event again (rule 3), which the review process hears more than once (BereanReview.md, *Risks*).

## 8. ProcessEntityModifiedAsync — step 4: Berean assigned and its review started (#1225)

1. **On every fact the Modified flow hears for a round that stands `Submitted`** — `-Modified`, `-Submitted` and `Association-Repointed`, a data change or not, and each a person's, since every ear drops a fact carrying the system identity (§ARC16.7.5; BereanReview.md rule 9, step 4; R4) — once the round has followed the entity, every dismissal the flow makes has run (*Order in the Modified flow*) and any data change's steps (§2) have run, and before the round is evaluated, the flow does as §7 rules 2 and 3 do: it assigns Berean where the policy asks for it automatically and no live assignment stands, and then, where one stands, publishes the review event once on a round that still offers Berean, or removes Berean from one that does not and publishes nothing (BereanReview.md rule 9, step 4.3; R9). A round that stands `Draft` after the flow has followed the entity runs none of it.
2. **The reset never reaches this step**, and this section adds no case for it. Its `-Submitted` carries the system identity and is dropped before the flow runs (§ARC16.7.5), and the owner ruled that the reset fires nothing (BereanReview.md rule 23; R4). The guard stays: lifted, it would let step 4.4 approve again the round the reset had just re-opened. *(This rule used to treat a `-Submitted` that found its round already `Submitted` as the reset's, assigning nothing on it and publishing the review event.)*
3. **So the review event is published again on a person's update while Berean stands assigned to a round that still offers it**, whatever the update was: under `RequireReapprovalOnChange = false` after a data change, which is what the owner asks (I4.4); on an echo; and on a submission from `Draft`. What the review process does with it is #1216's (BereanReview.md rule 17). On a round that no longer offers Berean the update removes Berean instead, so that the round no longer reads as waiting on a review nothing will start (R9).
4. **Its failures are §7 rule 5's**: a failed assignment or removal faults the flow before the evaluation, a collision with an assignment written in the same moment is contained as §7 rule 5 contains it, and a failed publish is logged.
5. **It replaces the automatic assignment's subscription on `Approval-Modified`**, which hears the same submissions (§APR8.6.2.1), and must reach `main` before that subscription is retired (`Backend/Orchestrations/AIReviewerOrchestrationService.BereanReview.md §6`; BereanReview.md, *What ships together* step 7). It does not wait for #1215: it asks nothing of whether the fact was a data change.
6. **The round is evaluated on conditions read after this step, under either setting** (*Order in the Modified flow* rule 4). Under `RequireReapprovalOnChange = true` the flow's read after the review dismissal already comes after it. Under `false` the flow today evaluates on the conditions it read to learn the setting, before this step; this section has it read them again once the step has run, and evaluate on that read, so that a round whose people's standing reviews meet the count is not approved on a read taken before Berean was assigned to it, nor held on one taken before Berean was removed from it. That retires the pin of the single read, `ShouldEvaluateOnTheConditionsAlreadyReadWhenTheStaleReviewResetIsOffAsync` (`ApprovalOrchestrationServiceTests.Flows.cs`), and rewrites the comment over the `false` path (`ApprovalOrchestrationService.Flows.cs`), as #1016 leaves it, which says the conditions already read are the ones to evaluate against.
