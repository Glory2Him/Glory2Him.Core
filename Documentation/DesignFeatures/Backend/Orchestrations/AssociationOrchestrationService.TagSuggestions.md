# Association orchestration service — tag suggestions
Parent: [TagSuggestions.md](../../TagSuggestions.md)
Level: orchestration — `IAssociationOrchestrationService` (`Glory2Him.Core/Services/Orchestrations/Associations/`), existing. Its other user stories are `Backend/Orchestrations/AssociationOrchestrationService.md`, the Likes feature's, and `AssociationOrchestrationService.RejectedEndpoints.md`.
Inherits: §ARC12.3.1 rules 5 and 5a, §ARC12.5 (*an orchestration holds brokers*, and the two-to-three rule), §ARC12.5.2 rule 6, §ARC16.2.1, §ARC16.8.1, §DOM4.4 rule 4, §DOM4.5, §DOM4.6, §SEC14.5 rules 2, 6 and 7, §SEC14.6, §SEC14.7 posture A′ rule 1, `Backend/Orchestrations/AssociationOrchestrationService.md` §1 rules 1–3 and §6, `AssociationOrchestrationService.RejectedEndpoints.md §1` rule 2, `Backend/Foundations/TagService.TagSuggestions.md` §1 and §3, TagSuggestions.md rules 1–15

The suggestion of a tag for a post by its name (TagSuggestions.md rule 1): the one member this feature adds. It finds the tag the name names or adds it, and pairs it with the post unless the pairing exists, reaching the rules that decide a pairing where this service already holds them. It is a third way in beside the upsert and the `Association-Adding` door, and is neither of them: the write-flow seam tests, which hold those two doors to one flow (`AssociationOrchestrationServiceTests.Substrate.WriteFlow.cs`), are unchanged by it.

## 1. SuggestTagAsync (needs issue)

```csharp
ValueTask<AssociationSubmissionResult> SuggestTagAsync(
    TagSuggestion tagSuggestion,
    CancellationToken cancellationToken = default);
```

A new member, direct only (TagSuggestions.md rule 12). Its models are new, under `Glory2Him.Core/Models/Orchestrations/Associations/`, and public, as the upsert's are, because `AssociationsController` binds them:

- **`TagSuggestion`** — the request: `ContentItemId`, a `Guid`, and `Name`, a `string`, and nothing else.
- **`AssociationSubmissionResult`** — the answer: `Status`, an `AssociationSubmissionStatus`, and nothing else (TagSuggestions.md rules 5 and 7). No id.
- **`AssociationSubmissionStatus`** — `Created`, `AlreadySuggested` and `AlreadySuggestedByCaller`, in that order. The numbers cross the wire, so a member is appended and none moves, as `AssociationSuggestionStatus`'s summary has it.

1. **The flow, in this order** (TagSuggestions.md rule 9). Each step refuses before the next is asked, and nothing is read or written past a refusal.
   1. **The request.** A `null` suggestion is refused, `NullAssociationOrchestrationException`, *"Tag suggestion is null."* The member then mints its envelope over the suggestion (`IEventEnvelopeBroker.CreateAsync`), as the upsert mints one over its working copy, and every gate below reads the security context from it, never from an ambient accessor (§ARC12.5.2 rule 6, *a read whose answer depends on who is asking is passed the envelope it is being made under*).
   2. **The contribution gate of an editorial pair**: authentication, and the global `ReadOnly` (§SEC14.7 posture A′ rule 1), as `ValidateUserIsAllowedToContribute` asks them of the upsert's editorial pair (`AssociationOrchestrationService.Validations.cs`). A refusal is `UnauthorizedAssociationOrchestrationException`.
   3. **The suggestion's fields**, in one validation (TagSuggestions.md rule 2): `ContentItemId` is not empty, *"Id is required"*; and `Name`, once trimmed of white space at both ends, is not empty, *"Text is required"*, and is at most 30 characters, *"Text exceed max length of 30 characters"*. A refusal is `InvalidAssociationOrchestrationException`, *"Tag suggestion is invalid, fix the errors and try again."*, naming `ContentItemId` or `Name` in its `Data`. Nothing strips a hash, and nothing changes letter case. Every step below reads the trimmed name.
   4. **The post, resolved as the upsert resolves an endpoint**: through `ResolveEndpointAsync` (`AssociationOrchestrationService.Endpoints.cs`), for a `ContentItem` endpoint keyed on `ContentItemId`, under the caller's envelope, so it reads `IContentItemService.RetrieveContentItemByIdAsync` as the ambient caller of this direct call (§DOM4.5). A post that does not exist, or that the caller may not see, is refused as resolution refuses one, `NotFoundAssociationOrchestrationException`, *"The content item endpoint was not found."* (§SEC14.5 rule 1).
   5. **The pair.** The post is endpoint A, carrying the group id, content type and scope resolution derived for it (§DOM4.5), and the tag is endpoint B, of type `Tag`, its key id and group id the tag's id once step 9 or 10 knows it, and its scope `EntityTypeVersioning.DefaultScopeFor(EntityType.Tag)`, as `DeriveEndpoint` derives a tag endpoint. `UserId` is `null`: the pair is editorial (§DOM4.10 rule 2). That order is canonical (§DOM4.4), and the foundation restores canonical order itself in any case (§DOM4.4 rule 4).
   6. **The pairing's endpoint veto**, over the pair, as `ValidateUserIsNotBlockedFromEndpoints` asks it of the upsert's editorial pair: `ContentItem-ReadOnly`, `ContentItem-{ContentType}-ReadOnly` for the post's derived type, and `Tag-ReadOnly` (§SEC14.7 posture A′ rule 1). It covers the tag's add's own veto, `ReadOnly` or `Tag-ReadOnly` (TagSuggestions.md rule 9, step 4). A refusal is `UnauthorizedAssociationOrchestrationException`.
   7. **A rejected post.** Where the post's `ApprovalStatus`, read by step 4 for the version `ContentItemId` names, is `Rejected`, the suggestion is refused, `InvalidAssociationOrchestrationException`, *"The content item is rejected, so a tag cannot be suggested for it."* (RejectedEndpoints.md rules 1 and 10). It reads the status `ResolvedEndpoint` keeps for an endpoint once #1358 has it kept (`AssociationOrchestrationService.RejectedEndpoints.md §1` rule 2), and reads nothing again.
   8. **The facet gate**, for the post as host and a `Tag` far end, as the upsert asks it (`ValidateSettingsAllowTheFacetAsync`, `AssociationOrchestrationService.FacetGate.cs`; §ARC16.2.1): the post's winning setting must carry `TagsAllowed`, and a post whose setting resolves no row is refused too. Each refusal is `InvalidAssociationOrchestrationException`, naming `TagsAllowed`, as the gate names it.
   9. **The read by name**: `ITagService.FindTagByNameAsync` with the trimmed name (`Backend/Foundations/TagService.TagSuggestions.md §3`). Where it answers a match whose `ApprovalStatus` is `Rejected`, deleted or not, or a match that is soft-deleted, the suggestion is refused, `InvalidAssociationOrchestrationException`, *"The tag cannot be suggested."*, one message for every such row, naming no status, no owner and no id (TagSuggestions.md rule 4; *Deviations* 2 there). Before it is thrown, the true reason is logged as information, naming the tag's id and whether it is rejected or soft-deleted, and noting that the caller was answered that the tag cannot be suggested (§SEC14.5 rules 6 and 7). Any other match is the tag the suggestion pairs, whatever its status and whoever owns it (TagSuggestions.md rules 4 and 5).
   10. **The add of a new tag**, where the read answers no match: `ITagService.AddTagAsync` with a new `Tag` carrying the trimmed name and `ApprovalStatus` `Submitted`, and nothing else (TagSuggestions.md rule 14). The add mints the tag's `Id` (`Backend/Foundations/TagService.TagSuggestions.md §1`), and stamps its audit fields under the caller, so the tag is the caller's. Where the add is refused as already holding the name — `TagDependencyValidationException` whose inner exception is `AlreadyExistsTagException` — the member reads the name once more, through step 9, and goes on with the match it finds as step 9 has it, a rejected or soft-deleted one refused; where that second read finds no match, the add's refusal is thrown on (TagSuggestions.md rule 10). It never adds twice.
   11. **The occupancy check**, the editorial arm's, over the pair with the tag's id on endpoint B: `FindPairOccupantsAsync` with no read envelope (`AssociationOrchestrationService.cs`), the exact-pair probe and then, where it finds no row, the overlap probe (TagSuggestions.md rule 6).
   12. **The insert of a free pair**, where neither probe finds a row: `IAssociationService.AddAssociationAsync` with the pair at `Submitted`, as the editorial arm hands its working copy to the add (`AssociationOrchestrationService.md §6` rule 3). The add mints the row's `Id`.
2. **The answer** (TagSuggestions.md rule 7):
   - **`Created`** where step 12 inserted the pair, whether the tag was found or added.
   - **`AlreadySuggestedByCaller`** where the exact-pair probe answered a row that is live, at `Draft` or `Submitted`, and whose `CreatedBy` is the caller's id. The caller's id is the envelope's `SecurityContext.SubjectId`, which is the id the security-audit broker stamps as `CreatedBy` (`SecurityContextPrincipalFactory.cs`), compared ordinally, as the foundations compare `CreatedBy` in their owner tests.
   - **`AlreadySuggested`** where either probe answered any other row: another caller's, at any status; one at `Approved` or `Rejected`, the caller's own included (TagSuggestions.md open question 1); a soft-deleted one; or a differently scoped live row the overlap probe found.

   It carries the status alone. Nothing is written where the pair is occupied.
3. **It writes nothing else, and publishes nothing of its own.** Its writes are the tag's add and the pairing's add, each publishing its own fact, `Tag-Added` and `Association-Added` (TagSuggestions.md rule 13). It writes nothing to a tag it finds.
4. **No catch arm is added.** Every member shares one `TryCatch` (`AssociationOrchestrationService.md §4` rule 9). Its own refusals are this service's validation family, through the arms it has: `NullAssociationOrchestrationException`, `UnauthorizedAssociationOrchestrationException`, `NotFoundAssociationOrchestrationException` and `InvalidAssociationOrchestrationException`, each mapped to `AssociationOrchestrationValidationException` and logged as an error (`CreateAndLogValidationExceptionAsync`). A refusal from `IAssociationService` is its dependency validation exception, and a failure its dependency exception, as for the upsert. Anything `ITagService` or `IContentItemService` raises past the steps above, and the facet gate's failed settings read, reach the arms they reach for the upsert: the closing `catch (Xeption)` as this service's dependency exception, and the closing `catch (Exception)` as its service exception (TagSuggestions.md rule 11).
5. **It takes a `CancellationToken`**, so a cancelled token is asked first, before the null check, as the upsert asks it, and a cancelled token and a timeout are answered through the `TryCatch`'s two cancellation arms (`AssociationOrchestrationService.Exceptions.cs`).
6. **A soft-deleted tag is refused by step 9 until #1362's undelete replaces that refusal on this route** (TagSuggestions.md rule 4; §ARC12.3.1 rule 5). #1362's design adds the section that does it, beside this one, and a soft-deleted tag that is also `Rejected` stays refused.
7. **The code text it adds is written with it.** The interface's summary of the member (`IAssociationOrchestrationService.cs`) says what it finds, adds and pairs, that a tag the caller cannot see is paired, what it refuses, and that it answers a status and no id. The class's summary (`AssociationOrchestrationService.cs`), which lists what the service does, gains the suggestion.

## Deviations

1. **The dependency count** — §ARC12.5, the two-to-three rule for an orchestration. This service holds seven entity services: `IAssociationService`, `IContentItemService`, `ITagService`, `IReactionService`, `IBibleReferenceService`, `ICommentService` and `ILinkService`. §1 adds one operation to it, and no dependency: it calls three of the seven. **Why:** the suggestion pairs a tag with a post, and the rules that decide a pairing — the post's resolution, the endpoint veto, the refusal of a rejected post, the facet gate and the occupancy check — are this service's, written once for the two doors that add a pairing (§ARC12.5.2 rule 6: *"Because the two doors share one write flow, a rule added to it later is on both by construction."*). A suggestion reaching them anywhere else would be a second copy of each. **What was rejected:**
   - **A service of its own**, holding `IContentItemService`, `ITagService` and `IAssociationService` — three, within two-to-three. It would copy every one of those rules and the endpoint resolution beneath them, and leave two copies to drift. And `AssociationsController` binds one service (§EVN13 rule 3; `the-standard-exposers` ts-exposers-001), so the suggestion's route would need a controller of its own, where the owner asked for the association controller to be extended (TagSuggestions.md, *Where the submission lives*).
   - **A processing service for the find-or-add, beneath this one.** A read by name followed by an add, over one entity, is a probe-then-write, which earns a processing service (§ARC12.1 rules 1 and 3). But this service reaches its entities through their foundation services, and an orchestration holds one kind of service, never a mix (§ARC12.5; `.claude/agents/planner.md`, *One kind of dependency, never a mix*). A tag processing service beside the six foundation services would mix them, and reaching every entity through a processing service would put a processing service over six entities none of whose flows earns one.
   - **The find-or-add in the tag foundation**, as one member. Higher-order logic composing an entity's foundation calls is processing work, not the foundation's (§ARC12.1 rule 1), and the member would answer a hidden row's id on the contract `TagsController` binds (§EVN13 rule 3; §SEC14.5 rules 1 and 3).

   The Likes user story's deviation (`AssociationOrchestrationService.md`, *Deviations* 1) names these seven for its own operations and is not this deviation's reason; nor is any other service's. §ARC12.5 entry 1's provisional note, which leaves open whether a processing service is earned beneath this service for the retrieve-or-add write, is unchanged. **Approved** by the owner on 2026-10-26 (TagSuggestions.md, *Both deviations are signed off*: *"The planner records both as approved by you, with today's date."*).
