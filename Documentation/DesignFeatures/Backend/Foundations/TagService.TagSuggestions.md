# Tag service — tag suggestions
Parent: [TagSuggestions.md](../../TagSuggestions.md)
Level: foundation — `ITagService` (`Glory2Him.Core/Services/Foundations/Tags/`), existing. Its other user stories are `Backend/Foundations/TagService.md`, `TagService.BereanReview.md`, `TagService.ConfidenceReads.md` and `TagService.SoftDeletion.md`.
Inherits: §APR9.7.1 rules 1 and 2, §ARC12.2.1 rules 1, 3, 5 and 6, §ARC12.3.1 rules 2a and 5, §ARC12.5 (*an orchestration holds brokers*), §EVN13 rule 3, §SEC14.5 rules 1, 3, 6 and 7, §SEC14.6 (rule 1, and *Cross-row rules under visibility filtering*), §SEC14.7 posture A rule 1, `Backend/Foundations/AssociationService.md` §13 and §14, TagSuggestions.md rules 2–4, 10, 11 and 13–15

What the tag foundation needs for a tag to be suggested by its name: an add that mints the new tag's `Id`, since the orchestration that builds the tag cannot (TagSuggestions.md rule 15), and a find-or-add that finds the tag a name is held by, whatever its state and whoever owns it, withholds a rejected or soft-deleted one, and adds the tag where no row holds the name (TagSuggestions.md rules 3, 4 and 10). §1 and §3 build them on the direct path, and §2 pins the mint on the `Tag-Adding` door. §1 changes an existing member, as `Backend/Foundations/AssociationService.md §13` records another feature's change to the association's add. §3 adds a member, departing from §ARC12.1 rule 1 by the owner's approval (*Deviations* 1).

**Build order.** §1, then §2. §3 after `Backend/Brokers/StorageBroker.TagSuggestions.md §1` and after §1, whose mint its add relies on, in either order with §2.

## 1. AddTagAsync — the add mints the row's `Id` (#1365)

```csharp
ValueTask<Tag> AddTagAsync(
    Tag tag,
    CancellationToken cancellationToken = default);
```

An existing member, changed: it stops taking the new row's `Id` from its caller. Its signature, its gate, its other validations, the write, the `Tag-Added` fact and its `ProcessedEvents` records are unchanged.

A tag's `Id` is an `IKey` member, so it belongs to the identifier broker and is never accepted from a caller (§APR9.7.1 rule 2), on the event path as well as the direct one (§APR9.7.1). Until this change the add took the `Id` from its caller and refused an empty one (`TagService.Validations.cs`, `ValidateOnAddTagAsync`). The suggestion's orchestration builds the tag that §3 adds through this member's write body, and cannot mint its `Id`, because an identifier broker is not among the brokers an orchestration holds (§ARC12.5, *an orchestration holds brokers*). So this service mints it, as the association's add does (`Backend/Foundations/AssociationService.md §13`). No deviation is needed.

1. **The add mints the new row's `Id`** through the identifier broker this service already holds (`TagService.cs`), and never takes the caller's. Whatever `Id` the caller's copy carries is overwritten, and is neither compared nor refused. The mint is in `DoAddTagAsync`, the private write body both entry paths share, and not in the public member, so it binds the `Tag-Adding` door too (§2).
2. **It mints after the contribution gate and before validation.** A caller the gate refuses mints nothing.
3. **The add's empty-`Id` rule is not asked**: the `Id` it would ask of is the one this service minted. Every other rule of `ValidateOnAddTagAsync` is asked as it is today.
4. **The added row, and the `Tag-Added` fact, carry the minted `Id`**, and so does what `AddTagAsync` returns. That is how a caller learns which row was written.
5. **`POST /api/Tags` answers the minted `Id`**, through `TagsController.PostTagAsync`, unchanged, which hands the add its body and answers `201` with the row the add returns. A caller's `Id` is ignored there. Tests that post a tag and then read it, or a pairing of it, by the `Id` they posted read it by the `Id` the post answered: the acceptance tests that post a tag (`Websites/Glory2Him.WebApp.Tests.Acceptance/Apis/Tags/`, and `Apis/Approvals/ApprovalTests.AutomaticAIReviewer.cs`), and the foundation's add tests (`Glory2Him.Core.Tests.Unit/Services/Foundations/Tags/TagServiceTests.Add.*.cs`).

## 2. OnAddingTagAsync — the minted `Id` on the `Tag-Adding` door (#1366)

```csharp
ValueTask<EventEnvelope<Tag>?> OnAddingTagAsync(
    EventEnvelope<Tag> envelope,
    CancellationToken cancellationToken = default);
```

An existing member, unchanged: the handler for `Tag-Adding` (`TagService.Substrate.cs`). It converges on `DoAddTagAsync`, so §1 binds it by construction. This section records what that means on this door, and proves it here, as `Backend/Foundations/AssociationService.md §14` proves the association's mint on its door.

1. **The row is written under the `Id` this service mints, never the one the publisher named.** §APR9.7.1 rule 2 holds on this path too, so no deviation is recorded. The envelope's content, which its signature covers (§SEC14.6 rule 4), is not refused for its `Id`, and the claim is neither compared nor refused, as on the direct path. The reply and the `Tag-Added` fact carry the minted `Id`.
2. **Its verification, its deduplication on `Metadata.EventId` and its contribution gate run before the mint**, so a replayed or tampered envelope, or a caller the gate refuses, mints nothing and writes nothing (§1 rule 2).
3. **Its tests are pins.** §1 puts the mint in the shared body, so they pass as soon as they are written, and they are committed as pins.

## 3. FindOrAddTagAsync (#1367)

```csharp
internal ValueTask<TagNameResult> FindOrAddTagAsync(
    Tag tag,
    CancellationToken cancellationToken = default);
```

A new member: the suggestion's find-or-add (TagSuggestions.md, *Splitting the suggestion* and *Where the find-or-add sits*). It finds the tag the handed tag's name is held by, whatever that tag's state and whoever owns it, withholds a rejected or soft-deleted one, and adds the handed tag where no row holds the name, so that what a name names is decided by a read no identity filters (TagSuggestions.md rules 3, 4 and 10). A read followed by a write over one entity is a processing service's under §ARC12.1 rule 1, and it is here by the owner's approval (*Deviations* 1).

1. **It is `internal` to Core**, as the identity-carrying `RetrieveTagByIdAsync` overload is (`ITagService.cs`), so it is no part of the contract `TagsController` is written against (§EVN13 rule 3), and no route serves it. Its one caller is `AssociationOrchestrationService.SuggestTagAsync` (`Backend/Orchestrations/AssociationOrchestrationService.TagSuggestions.md §1` rule 1, step 9). It has no event door: nothing publishes a request for it, as nothing publishes one for the association's pair probes.
2. **Its gate is the tag's contribution gate**, asked before anything is read: an authenticated caller holding neither `ReadOnly` nor `Tag-ReadOnly` (§SEC14.7 posture A rule 1; `ValidateUserIsAllowedToContribute`, `TagService.Validations.cs`). It asks it of the envelope it mints, which carries the ambient caller, as `FindAssociationByPairAsync` asks its gate on the method path (`AssociationService.Lookup.cs`): the member exists to serve a contribution, and carries its gate (§SEC14.6, *Cross-row rules under visibility filtering*). A caller it refuses is answered `UnauthorizedTagException`, wrapped as `TagValidationException`, and nothing is read or written.
3. **Its input is the tag to add, as handed.** A `null` tag is refused, `NullTagException` with *"Tag is null."*, wrapped as `TagValidationException`. Its name is required and at most 30 characters: a name that is `null`, empty or white space, or longer than 30 characters, is refused, `InvalidTagException` naming `Name`, wrapped as `TagValidationException`. Both are refused before anything is read. The member neither trims the name nor strips anything from it: what names a tag is the suggestion's to settle, and it hands this member the name it settled (TagSuggestions.md rule 2). The tag's other fields are the add's to judge, on the add path alone (rule 6): a tag that is found is answered as it stands, and the handed tag's status is never written to it (TagSuggestions.md rule 14).
4. **The read by name.** Its condition is authored here, as a query-shaping function, and handed to `IStorageBroker.SelectTagsAsync` (`Backend/Brokers/StorageBroker.TagSuggestions.md §1`; §ARC12.2.1 rules 1 and 3). The function uses `System.Linq` operators only (§ARC12.2.1 rule 3), and selects the rows whose `Name` equals the handed tag's name, by one term and no other: **no `IsDeleted` term, no approval or publication term and no owner term**. So it finds a soft-deleted row, a `Draft`, `Submitted`, `Approved` or `Rejected` row, an unpublished or scheduled row, and another caller's row, all alike. It projects each row to its `Id`, `ApprovalStatus` and `IsDeleted`, and reads no other column, and it shapes the query to one row: `IX_Tags_Name` is unique and unfiltered (`StorageBroker.Tag.Configurations.cs`), so a name is held by at most one row, soft-deleted rows included (§ARC12.3.1 rule 2a). It reads the same for every caller the gate admits: no caller term decides what it finds (§SEC14.6, *Cross-row rules under visibility filtering*).
5. **What it does with the row it finds** (TagSuggestions.md rule 4):
   - **A row that is neither `Rejected` nor soft-deleted**, whatever its status, its publication and its owner: the answer is `Found`, with the row's `Id`. Nothing is written.
   - **A `Rejected` row, soft-deleted or not, or a soft-deleted row: the tag is withheld.** The answer is `Withheld`, with the row's `Id`, and nothing is written: the handed tag is not added. Before the answer is returned, the true reason is logged as information, naming the tag's id and whether it is rejected or soft-deleted, and noting that it was withheld from the suggestion, which pairs nothing with it, and that the caller is not told so (§SEC14.5 rules 6 and 7). Nothing is thrown for it. A soft-deleted tag is withheld until #1362's undelete replaces that on this route (TagSuggestions.md rule 4; §ARC12.3.1 rule 5), and a soft-deleted tag that is also `Rejected` stays withheld.
   - **No row**: the handed tag is added (rule 6).
6. **The add of a new tag is the add's own.** The handed tag is written as `AddTagAsync` writes a tag, through the add's write body, `DoAddTagAsync`, and never a second copy of it: its gate, the mint of the row's `Id` (§1), its validations, its audit stamp under the caller, so that the tag is the caller's, the write, and the `Tag-Added` fact (TagSuggestions.md rules 13 and 15). The answer is `Added`, with the minted `Id`. A refusal of the add's validations is the add's, `TagValidationException`.
7. **A name taken between the read and the write** (TagSuggestions.md rule 10). Where the write is refused by `IX_Tags_Name` — the storage broker's duplicate-key refusal, which the add answers `AlreadyExistsTagException` — another caller has added the name since rule 4's read. The member reads the name once more, through rule 4's read, and answers the row it finds as rule 5 has it, a rejected or soft-deleted one withheld; it never writes twice. Where that second read finds no row, the refusal is thrown on as the add throws it, `TagDependencyValidationException` whose inner exception is `AlreadyExistsTagException`.
8. **`TagNameResult` carries the tag's `Id` and the member's `Outcome`, `Found`, `Added` or `Withheld`, and nothing else**: never the row's name, status, deletion, owner, publication fields, audit fields or confidence fields, so a withheld tag's reason stays in the log (rule 5; §SEC14.5 rules 1 and 3). Two new models under `Glory2Him.Core/Models/Foundations/Tags/`: `TagNameResult`, a public class as `AssociationPairMatch` is (`Glory2Him.Core/Models/Foundations/Associations/AssociationPairMatch.cs`), and `TagNameOutcome`, the enum of the three outcomes in that order.
9. **Letter case is the column's**, compared in SQL under the column's collation, so a name that differs from the stored one only in letter case finds it (TagSuggestions.md rule 2). An in-memory evaluation compares ordinally and cannot prove it, so an integration test proves it against the real catalogue, with the shaped query's translation and its projection (§ARC12.2.1 rule 6), in `Glory2Him.Core.Tests.Integration/Services/Foundations/Tags/`, as `AssociationNarrowReadTests` proves the association's narrow reads (`Glory2Him.Core.Tests.Integration/Services/Foundations/Associations/`).
10. **Its unit tests execute the condition** (§ARC12.2.1 rule 5): the mocked storage broker applies the function it is handed to an in-memory set holding the row the name names and a row whose name differs, and, for each term the condition must not have, a row that meets the name and differs on that term alone — soft-deleted, at each other approval status, unpublished or scheduled, another caller's.
11. **Its failures are the service's**: a storage failure is `TagDependencyException`, an unexpected one `TagServiceException`, through the service's `TryCatch` (`TagService.Exceptions.cs`); and a cancelled token and a timeout are answered as the service's other members answer them, since the member takes a `CancellationToken` (`the-standard-cancellation-patterns`).

## Deviations

1. **§ARC12.1 rule 1, and `the-standard-processings` — a find-or-add in a foundation.** §3 reads the row a name is held by and, where none is, adds one: a probe-then-write over one entity, which §ARC12.1 rule 1 gives to a processing service (*"retrieve-then-branch, probe-then-write"*), as `the-standard-processings` gives a processing service its higher-order operations that combine foundation calls (ts-processings-003, *EnsureStudentExistsAsync*). **Why:** the owner ruled that the find-or-add, with its withholding, becomes an operation of its own beneath the suggestion (TagSuggestions.md, *Splitting the suggestion*), because the suggestion had grown past the planner's size gate. Beneath `AssociationOrchestrationService` there are two places for it, and this is the one that keeps the orchestration on the seven foundation services its own deviation was approved for (`Backend/Orchestrations/AssociationOrchestrationService.TagSuggestions.md`, *Deviations* 1). **Instead:** the member is `internal`, so no route reaches it and no row the caller may not see reaches the contract `TagsController` binds (rule 1); it answers an id and an outcome alone (rule 8); and it writes a new tag through the add's own write body, so the add's gate, mint, validations and fact are written once (rule 6). **What was rejected:** a tag processing service, The Standard's home for the operation, which would have given the orchestration an eighth entity service and mixed foundation and processing services, which the root `CLAUDE.md` *Non-negotiables* bar (*"never a mix of foundation and processing"*); and leaving the find-or-add in the orchestration, which the size ruling refused. **Approved** by the owner on 2026-10-29 (TagSuggestions.md, *Where the find-or-add sits*: *"A member of ITagService, hidden from the tags API. The orchestration keeps its approved seven services. Departs from §ARC12.1 rule 1 and The Standard, which put find-or-add in processing. #1367 is folded in and re-carved."*). No other service's deviation is its reason.
